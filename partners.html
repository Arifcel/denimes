How the network shows up for founders

Field access

Time on a factory floor, a trading desk or a hospital ward, so product decisions begin with how the work is actually done.

Candid reviews

Twice a year, two or three partners read a company’s plan cold and tell the founders what they would change before the market does.

Regulatory context

Partners who have been through licensing, audits and certification explain what is coming next, and in which order to tackle it.

Market entry

When a company opens in a new country, partners already there share local contacts, norms and the missteps they have watched others make.
